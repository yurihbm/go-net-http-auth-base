## Reporting a Vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for security problems.

- Preferred: use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the "Report a vulnerability" button in the repository's Security tab).
- Alternative: email `security@example.com` (placeholder, replace with a real contact).

Include the affected endpoint or component, steps to reproduce and the impact you observed.
